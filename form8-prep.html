Form 8

Official form

Correction of entries

For voters already on the electoral roll.

Documents required

Open the one that applies to you. You will choose it at the start of the form.

Details you can correct

Up to 4 per application
  • Name
  • Gender
  • Date of birth or age
  • Relation typeFather, mother, husband, wife or guardian
  • Relative’s name
  • AddressMistakes only
  • Mobile number
  • Photograph
info
Correct your address only to fix a mistake, such as a wrong house number or spelling. If you have moved, choose Move to a new address instead.
  • check

    One self-attested document for each detail

    • Birth certificate from a municipal authority or registrar
    • Class X or XII certificate showing date of birth
    • PAN card
    • Driving licence
    • Water, electricity or gas bill for the address, at least one year
    • Registered sale deed or rent agreement
    • Land records, including Kisan Bahi
    • Current bank or post office passbook
    • Indian passport
    • Aadhaar card
  • check

    A new photograph, if correcting it

    Colour, unsigned, 4.5 cm by 3.5 cm, white background.

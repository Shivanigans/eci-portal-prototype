Form 6

Official form

Registration as a new voter

You can register as a voter if you are a citizen of India, are 18 or older on the qualifying date, and ordinarily live in the constituency where you want to register. The qualifying dates are 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October.

Keep the following documents handy to keep the process smooth.

  • Passport-size photograph

    A recent colour photograph against a white background, with the full face looking at the camera.

    JPG or JPEG, under 2 MB

  • Date of birth proof

    A Self-attested means you sign the copy yourself before uploading it. copy of one document showing your date of birth.

    See accepted documents
    • Aadhaar card
    • PAN card
    • Indian passport
    • Driving licence
    • Birth certificate
    • A Class 10 or Class 12 certificate that shows the date of birth

    JPG, PNG or PDF, under 2 MB

  • Address proof

    A Self-attested means you sign the copy yourself before uploading it. copy of one document showing that you live at the address you are registering.

    See accepted documents
    • Aadhaar card
    • Indian passport
    • A bank or post office passbook currently in use
    • A water, electricity or gas bill at least one year old
    • Land records, including Kisan Bahi
    • A registered rent or lease deed
    • A registered sale deed

    JPG, PNG or PDF, under 2 MB

  • Mobile phone

    A mobile number you can access while filling the form, to receive a one-time password (OTP).

What happens next

  1. 1 Reference number You receive a reference number, which you use to track the application.
  2. 2 Field verification A booth level officer verifies your details, and may visit your address.
  3. 3 Decision The electoral registration officer decides on the application.
  4. 4 Added to the roll If approved, your name is added to the electoral roll and your voter ID is issued.

Applications are usually decided within 30 days, as set out in the Registration of Electors Rules 1960, and timelines can vary during election periods. Track an application

Common queries

Am I already on the electoral roll?

If your record is already on the roll, you do not need to submit Form 6. Search the electoral roll.

I have moved house, what should I do?

Changes of address and corrections to an existing record are made on a different form. Go to Form 8.

I live abroad, can I still register?

Overseas electors register on a separate form using their Indian passport. Go to Form 6A.

Can I apply on paper instead?

The form can be filled by hand and given to your electoral registration officer or booth level officer, who also supply it free of charge. Download Form 6.

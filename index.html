    Common questions

    Do I need a voter ID to vote?

    No. You can vote with any government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, driving licence, PAN card, and others) as long as your name appears on the electoral roll for your polling station.

    Check if your name is on the roll →
    How do I know if my name is on the voter list?

    You can search by your EPIC number, personal details, or mobile number. You’ll see your name, polling station, and constituency if you’re registered.

    Search the electoral roll →
    How do I correct a mistake on my voter ID?

    You can update your name, address, photo, or other details. You’ll need to log in and upload supporting documents.

    Start a correction →
    What documents do I need to register?

    You’ll need one proof of age (birth certificate, school leaving certificate, passport, or PAN card) and one proof of address (utility bill, rent agreement, Aadhaar, or bank passbook). Documents can be uploaded as JPG, PNG, or PDF, maximum 2 MB each.

What happens next

  1. 1 Field verification A booth level officer (BLO) verifies your details, and may visit your address.
  2. 2 Decision The electoral registration officer (ERO) decides on the application.
  3. 3 Added to the roll If approved, your name is added to the electoral roll and your voter ID is issued.

Applications are usually decided within 30 days, as set out in the Registration of Electors Rules 1960, and timelines can vary during election periods.
